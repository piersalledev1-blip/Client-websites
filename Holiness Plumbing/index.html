ABOUT HOLINESS

About Holiness Plumbing & Construction

Holiness Plumbing & Construction is a licensed plumbing and construction company serving residential, commercial and industrial customers across Ghana. Serving Ghana since 2016, we provide professional supply, installation, maintenance and repair services for plumbing systems and plumbing-related construction projects.

Our services cover complete plumbing installations, drainage systems, bathroom installations, water systems, biodigester and biogas solutions, septic systems and industrial plumbing. We also supply plumbing materials and equipment and provide professional installation for suitable projects.

Holiness Plumbing & Construction focuses on practical plumbing solutions and reliable workmanship for homes, businesses and industrial facilities across Ghana, with primary service coverage in Accra and Kumasi.
